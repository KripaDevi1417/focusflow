How it works

The next 25 minutes are sacred. Everything else is admin.

FocusFlow does not try to run your whole life. It gets the thought out of your head, shrinks the task until starting is easy, and then protects one block of attention.

  • Capture the thought before it becomes background anxiety.
  • Pick one task. The rest wait, visibly, in the list.
  • Hit shield: fullscreen, one clock, tab title counting down.
  • Log the urge when it arrives - noticing beats resisting.
  • Finish with a session in the record and a next step written down.

Straight answers

Questions people actually ask.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes, forever. It caps open tasks at 10, sessions at 4 a day, history at 7 days and goals at 3. No card, no trial countdown, no downgrade surprise.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Sign in with Google or an email code, and the free plan is live immediately. A card only appears when you choose a paid plan.

How does sign-in work without a password?

You enter a username and email, then a six-digit code is sent to that inbox. With Supabase connected, the code is emailed by Supabase. Google sign-in is one tap. There is no password to reuse or leak.

Where does my data live?

In this build, per-account on your device - isolated by a key that includes your account id. The included Supabase schema is ready for Postgres with row-level security, so when sync ships no other user can read your rows. You can export or delete everything at any time.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing punitive. The streak shows the truth, and a repair for Pro is on the roadmap so one bad day does not erase a good month. The point is returning, not perfection.

Can I cancel or export my data?

Cancel whenever - access lasts to the end of the paid period. Export a JSON backup at any time, a session CSV on Plus and above, and delete your data in two clicks.
